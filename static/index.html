  4. Find and copy the value of sp_dc, then paste it above!

Privacy, Terms & Legal

🛡️ Privacy Policy (100% Client-Side)

Your data never leaves your device: Spoti-Less operates on a zero-server-tracking architecture. Your Spotify OAuth tokens, game statistics, streaks, and theme preferences are saved exclusively in your browser's private localStorage.

We do not operate tracking databases, sell personal data, or run advertising pixels or third-party tracking scripts.

📜 Terms of Service & Fair Use Disclaimer

Spoti-Less is an independent, open-source, non-commercial fan-made trivia game created for educational and entertainment purposes. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Spotify AB or Lessgames.

Short music preview clips (0.1s to 5.0s) and album art thumbnails are fetched via official public developer APIs solely for identification and commentary under non-commercial Fair Use guidelines.

🍪 Cookie, Fee & Refund Policy

Zero Cookies & 100% Free: This app does not use marketing cookies. Spoti-Less is completely free: there are no paid tiers, subscriptions, hidden fees, or in-app purchases (hence no refund policy applies).

👶 Age Notice & Contact

Spoti-Less does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under 13. This project is open source on GitHub.

🗑️ Data Deletion & Right to Erasure

You can wipe all stored tokens, playlist caches, theme preferences, and game statistics from your browser at any time in Settings, or by clearing your browser site data.
