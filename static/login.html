Paste any Spotify playlist link from your phone or PC, or paste your sp_dc cookie from desktop Chrome/Edge.

How to copy a playlist link or sp_dc cookie?
  1. Mobile & PC (Playlist Link): Open Spotify → go to a playlist → tap Share → Copy Link, then paste it above. (Note: Live personal Spotify streaming runs on the Desktop Native app; the Vercel web site plays Cloud Top Hits.)
  2. PC Desktop (sp_dc Cookie): Sign in at open.spotify.com, press F12 → Application > Cookies → copy sp_dc.
