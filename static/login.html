🎵 Connect Spotify via Username or Playlist (Mobile & PC)

Just paste your Spotify username, profile link, or any public playlist link — no password or cookie needed! Note: Your playlists must be set to Public on Spotify.

⚠️ Heads up on Niche / Spotify-Exclusive Songs: On the web, Spoti-Less plays your songs using Spotify Previews, iTunes, Deezer, and YouTube. If an extremely niche or Spotify-exclusive track isn't available on any web source, Spoti-Less will warn you and auto-skip to the next song without hurting your streak!
How to make a playlist Public or copy your Spotify link?
  1. Username / Profile Link: Open Spotify → Profile → tap Share → Copy Link (or type your username/ID), and make sure the playlists you want to play are set to Public (Add to profile).
  2. Single Playlist Link: Open any public playlist → tap Share → Copy Link and paste it above.
  3. PC Desktop (sp_dc Cookie): Optional for local desktop app — sign in at open.spotify.com, press F12 → Application > Cookies → copy sp_dc.
